Tournament Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Crossword Tournament platform collects, uses, and safeguards information when you participate in our crossword tournaments.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data necessary to facilitate competition integrity, scoring, and leaderboard display:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth 2.0, we receive your email address, default profile name, and a unique Google identifier (UID).
  • Participant Profile: The tournament nickname you choose during registration and your selected competition division.
  • Competition Activity & Submissions: For each tournament puzzle, we record your puzzle start and finish timestamps, total elapsed seconds, calculated score breakdown, and submitted letter grid.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use your browser's localStorage solely for functional purposes:
    • Auto-saving your in-progress grid and elapsed timer during active solves to preserve game state across accidental window reloads.
    • Tracking completed warm-up puzzles locally.
    • Remembering your solving and display preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to administer the tournament:

  • Verifying that your email address is on the tournament's authorized participant whitelist.
  • Tracking puzzle progress, calculating scores, and ranking solvers within divisions.
  • Allowing tournament administrators to verify competition integrity, audit submission grids, and resolve scoring disputes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your information for advertising or commercial marketing.

3. Visibility & Information Sharing

  • Public / Participant Leaderboard: Your chosen nickname, division, score, elapsed time, and clean-solve statistics are visible to other authorized participants and organizers on the tournament standings page.
  • Administrative Access: Only authorized tournament administrators have access to the participant whitelist and administrative management tools.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

The tournament relies on the following third-party infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Google Firebase: Provides secure authentication (Firebase Auth) and database hosting (Cloud Firestore). Data is processed in accordance with Google's Cloud Privacy Notice.
  • Web Hosting Provider: Static web assets are served via secure static hosting (such as GitHub Pages or secure web servers).

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Competition records and submission logs are retained during the event and for a reasonable dispute-resolution and archiving period following the tournament's conclusion. If you wish to request the deletion or anonymization of your participant profile and score history, please contact your tournament administrator.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the tournament organizing committee.
