Tournament Rules & Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to the Crossword Tournament. By participating in this tournament, accessing the solver interface, or registering a participant profile, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Tournament Rules and Terms of Service.

1. Eligibility & Authentication

Participation is restricted to authorized solvers registered by tournament administrators. To access tournament puzzles and record scores, you must authenticate using your authorized Google Account. Each participant may only register and compete with a single account.

2. Fair Play & Competition Integrity

All participants agree to uphold the spirit of honest and fair competition:

  • No External Assistance: Solvers must complete all tournament puzzles independently without dictionaries, search engines, anagram tools, crossword solvers, bots, or assistance from other individuals, unless explicitly authorized by the specific division rules (e.g., designated Pairs divisions).
  • No Client Tampering: Tampering with client-side code, scripts, local timers, network requests, or database records is strictly prohibited.
  • Single Attempt: Each puzzle may only be started and submitted once. Leaving or closing a puzzle window will not pause or reset your elapsed competition time.

3. Leaderboards & Public Display of Results

By registering and competing, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • Your chosen leaderboard nickname, division, total score, elapsed solving time, and clean-solve statistics will be displayed on the tournament standings page to other participants and organizers.
  • Final results, rankings, and anonymized submission metrics may be archived or exported by tournament organizers for official records and post-event reporting.

4. Scoring, Auditing & Organizer Authority

Scoring is determined according to the tournament's scoring parameters (including correct word points, time bonuses, and overtime penalties). Tournament administrators retain full discretion and final authority to:

  • Review and audit submitted grids and solve timelines.
  • Adjust scores or apply penalties in the event of timing anomalies, verified technical errors, or rule violations.
  • Disqualify any participant who breaches these rules or engages in unsporting conduct.

5. Technical Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

While we strive to provide a seamless competition experience, the tournament platform is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. Tournament organizers and platform developers are not liable for:

  • Local internet connection drops, latency, or Wi-Fi interruptions during timed solves.
  • Device failures, operating system crashes, or browser-specific issues.
  • Temporary third-party service interruptions (such as Google Authentication or cloud infrastructure).

6. Privacy

Our collection and handling of participant data is governed by our Privacy Policy. Please review the Privacy Policy to understand how your information is handled.

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding these rules, require score review assistance, or need technical support, please contact your tournament administrator or director.
